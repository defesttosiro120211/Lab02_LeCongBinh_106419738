COS10026 Computing Technology Inquiry Project

Unit Outline - Semester 1, 2025

Unit Details

Credit Points: 12.5
Teaching Period: Semester 1, 2025
Mode of Delivery: Blended learning / On campus
Prerequisites: None

Aims and Objectives

This unit aims to provide students with foundational skills in inquiry-based project work within computing technology. Students work in groups to solve problems, develop digital solutions, and apply core principles of web technologies and professional communications.

Unit Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of this unit, students will be able to:

  1. Apply introductory knowledge of web systems, HTML, and structured document design.
  2. Collaborate effectively in teams to investigate computing problems and present solutions.
  3. Demonstrate foundational inquiry techniques to solve real-world technology challenges.
  4. Communicate technical ideas clearly in both oral and written formats.

Learning Resources

Materials and weekly tutorial sheets will be provided via the university learning management system. For further university academic guides, visit Swinburne University of Technology.
